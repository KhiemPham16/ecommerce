SRS BOOKSTORE / BOOKSTORY
Software Requirements Specification

Phien ban tai lieu: 2.0
Ngay cap nhat: 08/06/2026

============================================================
1. GIOI THIEU
============================================================

1.1 Muc dich
Tai lieu SRSBookStore mo ta yeu cau phan mem cho he thong thuong mai dien tu ban sach BookStore/BookStory. Tai lieu nay dung de thong nhat pham vi chuc nang, vai tro nguoi dung, yeu cau du lieu, yeu cau giao dien va tieu chi chap nhan cua du an.

He thong bao gom:
- Giao dien khach hang.
- Khu vuc tai khoan nguoi dung.
- Dashboard quan tri.
- Cac module quan ly san pham, danh muc, don hang, nhan vien, khach hang, bai viet, media, ma giam gia va phuong thuc thanh toan.

1.2 Pham vi
He thong cho phep khach hang:
- Xem trang chu bang du lieu that tu backend.
- Xem danh muc san pham.
- Tim kiem san pham.
- Xem chi tiet san pham.
- Them san pham vao gio hang.
- Mua ngay va dieu huong den gio hang/thanh toan.
- Doc blog va chi tiet blog.
- Dang ky, dang nhap, quen mat khau, dat lai mat khau.
- Quan ly tai khoan va xem lich su don hang.
- Danh gia san pham neu da mua san pham.

He thong cho phep nguoi dung noi bo:
- Truy cap dashboard theo dung quyen.
- Quan ly du lieu van hanh cua cua hang.
- An cac tinh nang khong co quyen tren giao dien.
- Chan truy cap truc tiep URL dashboard neu khong du quyen.

1.3 Doi tuong su dung
- Khach hang: xem sach, tim kiem, dat hang, doc blog, quan ly tai khoan, danh gia san pham da mua.
- Nhan vien: quan ly san pham, bai viet, media, xem don hang, cap nhat status va paymentStatus don hang.
- Quan ly: quan ly cac module van hanh, tao nhan vien moi, nhung khong duoc tu thay doi quyen ban than va khong duoc chi dinh nguoi khac lam MANAGER neu khong du quyen.
- Quan tri vien: co toan quyen tren dashboard, bao gom phan quyen nhan vien va quan ly.

1.4 Dinh nghia viet tat
- SRS: Software Requirements Specification.
- FE: Frontend.
- BE: Backend.
- API: Application Programming Interface.
- CRUD: Create, Read, Update, Delete.
- JWT: JSON Web Token.
- UI: User Interface.
- UX: User Experience.

============================================================
2. MO TA TONG QUAN HE THONG
============================================================

2.1 Kien truc tong quan
- Frontend: React, React Router, Zustand, Axios, SCSS Module, Vite.
- Backend: Node.js, Express, Prisma, REST API.
- Database: phu thuoc cau hinh backend hien tai.
- Xac thuc: access token, refresh token va API /users/me.
- Media: upload va quan ly file qua route media cua backend.
- Thanh toan: ho tro trang thai thanh toan va luong xac nhan thanh toan theo backend.

2.2 Cac phan he chinh
- Public site: Home, Category, Product Details, Blog, Blog Detail, Contact.
- User flow: Auth, Forgot Password, Reset Password, Account, Account Orders, Cart, Pay, Sepay, Payment Confirmation.
- Dashboard: Overview, Employees, Products, Categories, Customers, Orders, Blogs/Posts, Media, Coupons, Payment Methods.
- Shared FE layer: services, stores, utils, reusable form components, media picker, protected route.

2.3 Nguyen tac thiet ke frontend
- Page dashboard nen tach theo module cha.
- Service xu ly viec goi API.
- Store xu ly state, loading, saving va action bat dong bo.
- Form CRUD nen tach rieng thanh component/page con trong folder module.
- UI khong hien nut/chuc nang neu nguoi dung khong co quyen.
- Route dashboard phai co guard chan truy cap truc tiep.
- Cac nut trong form create/edit phai co noi dung ro rang nhu "Xac nhan".
- Mau chu cua button phai dam bao doc duoc, khong bi trang tren nen trang.
- Mau chu dao cua cac nut mua/them gio hang la #2A83E9.

3.2 Quyen dashboard tong quan

- MANAGER duoc truy cap cac module van hanh chinh va co the tao nhan vien moi theo gioi han quyen.

- CUSTOMER khong duoc truy cap dashboard.

3.3 Quyen dashboard chi tiet
- Overview: ADMIN, MANAGER.
- Employees: ADMIN, MANAGER.
- Products: ADMIN, MANAGER, EMPLOYEE.
- Categories: ADMIN, MANAGER.
- Customers: ADMIN, MANAGER.
- Orders: ADMIN, MANAGER, EMPLOYEE.
- Blogs/Posts: ADMIN, MANAGER, EMPLOYEE.
- Media: ADMIN, MANAGER, EMPLOYEE.
- Coupons: ADMIN, MANAGER.
- Payment Methods: ADMIN, MANAGER.

3.4 Quyen quan ly nhan vien
- ADMIN co the tao, cap nhat, khoa/xoa va phan quyen nhan vien.
- ADMIN co the gan role EMPLOYEE hoac MANAGER cho nguoi dung noi bo theo quy tac he thong.
- MANAGER co the tao nhan vien moi theo gioi han he thong.
- MANAGER khong duoc chi dinh nhan vien khac lam MANAGER neu khong du quyen.

3.5 An tinh nang theo quyen
- Sidebar dashboard chi hien thi module ma role hien tai duoc phep truy cap.
- Neu nguoi dung go truc tiep URL dashboard khong co quyen, he thong chuyen ve trang dashboard dau tien ma nguoi do duoc phep truy cap.
- Tieu de dashboard phai lay theo module duoc phep, khong hien module bi cam.
- Cac nut thao tac nhay cam phai an neu role khong du quyen.

4. YEU CAU CHUC NANG - XAC THUC VA TAI KHOAN
============================================================

4.1 Dang ky
- Khach hang co the tao tai khoan bang ho ten, email, so dien thoai va mat khau.
- Sau khi dang ky, he thong thong bao kiem tra email neu backend yeu cau xac minh.

4.2 Dang nhap
- Nguoi dung co the dang nhap bang email va mat khau.
- Sau khi dang nhap thanh cong, FE luu accessToken va thong tin user vao store.
- He thong goi API /users/me de lay thong tin nguoi dung hien tai.
- Neu user co role noi bo va truy cap dashboard, ProtectedRoute kiem tra role.

4.3 Dang xuat
- Nguoi dung co the dang xuat.
- Sau khi dang xuat, FE xoa accessToken, user va storage lien quan.

4.4 Xac minh email
- He thong ho tro trang verify email theo token/duong dan backend.
- Khi thanh cong, hien thong bao xac minh thanh cong.

4.5 Quen mat khau
- Nguoi dung co the nhap email de yeu cau dat lai mat khau.
- He thong gui yeu cau den backend.
- FE hien thong bao huong dan kiem tra email/OTP.

4.6 Dat lai mat khau
- Nguoi dung co the nhap email, OTP/token va mat khau moi.
- FE gui yeu cau reset password den backend.
- Neu thanh cong, nguoi dung co the quay lai trang dang nhap.

4.7 Quan ly tai khoan
- Khach hang co the xem/cap nhat thong tin ca nhan neu backend ho tro.
- Khach hang co the xem lich su don hang.
- Khach hang co the xem chi tiet tung don hang.

5. YEU CAU CHUC NANG - GIAO DIEN KHACH HANG
============================================================

5.1 Header
- Logo tren header phai dieu huong ve trang chu.
- Header su dung logo dong bo voi footer.
- Header co o tim kiem san pham.
- Nut search su dung icon search cua React/lucide neu du an co thu vien icon.
- Khi tim kiem, he thong dieu huong sang trang Category voi keyword tuong ung.
- Nut gio hang dieu huong sang /cart.
- Header phai hien thi dung trang thai dang nhap/dang xuat.
- Neu user co quyen dashboard, co the co lien ket vao dashboard theo thiet ke.
- Khong tu dong dua ADMIN vao dashboard khi bam vao san pham o giao dien khach hang; thao tac tren public site phai giu dung luong public site tru khi nguoi dung bam Dashboard.

5.2 Footer
- Logo footer phai dieu huong ve trang chu.
- Footer lay danh muc that tu backend.
- Link danh muc trong footer dieu huong dung sang trang danh muc voi category thuc te.
- Neu backend khong co danh muc, hien thi fallback hop ly.

5.3 Trang chu
- Trang chu su dung du lieu that tu backend.
- Hien thi danh muc noi bat/active.
- Hien thi san pham noi bat, san pham moi hoac san pham dang ban.
- Hien thi bai viet moi nhat neu co.
- San pham tren trang chu co the dieu huong sang Product Details.
- Danh muc tren trang chu dieu huong sang Category theo categoryId/slug.
- UI trang chu phai co khoang cach hop ly, dong bo voi mau chu dao cua du an.

5.4 Trang danh muc san pham
- Lay danh muc that tu backend.
- Lay san pham that tu backend.
- Ho tro loc theo danh muc.
- Ho tro tim kiem theo keyword tu header.
- Moi card san pham hien thi anh, ten sach, tac gia, danh muc, gia, ton kho/trang thai neu can.
- Moi card san pham co vien xanh mong theo mau chu dao.
- Moi card co nut them vao gio hang bang icon.
- Moi card co nut mua bang icon hoac icon + text.
- Khi bam "Them vao gio hang", san pham duoc them vao gio hang.
- Khi bam "Mua", san pham duoc them vao gio hang va dieu huong sang /cart de tien hanh thanh toan.
- Nut mua/them gio hang dung mau #2A83E9.
- Bam vao phan san pham dieu huong sang Product Details.

5.5 Trang chi tiet san pham
- Route chi tiet san pham la /product/:slug hoac theo cau hinh hien tai cua FE.
- Trang lay du lieu san pham that tu backend.
- Hien thi anh, ten sach, tac gia, nha xuat ban, ISBN, danh muc, gia, ton kho, mo ta.
- Cho phep chon so luong trong gioi han ton kho.
- Co nut "Them vao gio hang".
- Co nut "Mua ngay"; khi bam se them vao gio hang va dieu huong sang /cart.
- Nut hanh dong dung mau chu dao #2A83E9.
- Hien thi danh sach danh gia cua san pham.
- Chi nguoi da mua san pham moi duoc danh gia.
- Nguoi da danh gia co the sua lai danh gia cua minh neu backend cho phep.
- Hien thi san pham goi y cung danh muc.
- San pham goi y cung danh muc hien thi toi da 4 san pham.
- Card san pham goi y phai hien thi ro anh, ten sach va thong tin chinh, khong bi anh che title.
- Neu san pham khong ton tai, hien thi trang thai khong tim thay.

5.6 Gio hang
- Gio hang hien thi danh sach san pham da them.
- Co the tang/giam so luong, xoa san pham khoi gio hang.
- Tinh tong tien, so luong va thong tin thanh toan.
- Tu gio hang co the dieu huong sang trang thanh toan.

5.7 Thanh toan
- Trang Pay hien thi thong tin don hang, dia chi, phuong thuc thanh toan va tong tien.
- Sau khi dat hang, he thong tao don hang theo API backend.
- Neu phuong thuc thanh toan yeu cau xac nhan, dieu huong sang Sepay hoac Payment Confirmation theo flow hien tai.
- He thong hien trang thai thanh toan cho nguoi dung.

5.8 Contact
- Trang contact phai co layout dong bo voi cac trang khac.
- Khoang trong hai ben can can doi, khong bi rong qua muc so voi cac trang public site khac.
